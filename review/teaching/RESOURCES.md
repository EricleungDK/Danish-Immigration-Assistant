# Semantic Answer Adjudication Resources

## Knowledge

- [Evaluation quality bar](../../docs/evaluation-quality-bar.md)
  Authoritative review, replay, and release-threshold contract. Use for deciding
  what evidence is required and how the accepted bundle is validated.
- [Canonical packet G instructions](../../README.md)
  Exact private paths and SHA-256 values. Use before loading or replaying packet G.
- [Semantic adjudication workspace](../semantic-adjudication-review.html)

## Wisdom (Communities)

No external community is needed for this private, exact-bound review. The human
reviewer must personally judge the packet rather than outsource the decisions.
